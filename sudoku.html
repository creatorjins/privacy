Article 3 (User Rights and Opt-Out)

Users can restrict or disable personalized advertising by adjusting their mobile device settings at any time:

  • Android: Settings > Privacy > Ads > Delete Advertising ID / Opt out of Ads Personalization

Since this App does not collect or store personal data or account information on external servers, there is no personal user data stored by us to delete. Game progress is saved locally on your device and can be removed by clearing the app data or uninstalling the app.
